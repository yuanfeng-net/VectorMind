# Contributing to VectorMind

Bug reports, reproducible test cases, security reports, and design proposals
are welcome through the official repository.

Do not submit source-code contributions unless the VectorMind Licensor has
first provided a written contributor agreement for that contribution. This
keeps the copyright and licensing rights needed to protect VectorMind from
unauthorized redistribution clear and enforceable.

An unsolicited pull request does not grant VectorMind permission to use the
submitted code and may be closed without review. When a written contributor
agreement applies, that agreement controls the ownership and licensing of the
accepted contribution.

By submitting issue text, logs, or a minimal reproduction, You confirm that You
have the right to share it and permit the project to use it for diagnosis,
testing, documentation, and maintenance. Do not include secrets, credentials,
personal data, or third-party confidential information.
