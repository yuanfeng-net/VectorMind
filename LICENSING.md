private modifications, authorized contractors, cloud infrastructure, and CI/CD.

operation of VectorMind as a third-party product or hosted service.

To request redistribution, OEM, hosted-service, resale, or other additional
rights:

1. Open a licensing request through the official repository at
   <https://github.com/yuanfeng-net/VectorMind/issues> or use the current
   contact method published by the owner of
   <https://github.com/yuanfeng-net/VectorMind>.
2. Identify the requesting individual or legal entity, intended use,
   distribution method, expected users, and whether the use is commercial.
3. Wait for a separate written agreement or explicit written authorization
   from the VectorMind Licensor.

Opening an issue, sending a request, receiving an automated response, or
silence from the Licensor does not grant permission. Authorization is valid
only when it identifies the requesting party and the rights granted.

Suspected unauthorized copies or packages may be reported through the same
official repository. Include the copied repository or package URL and the
matching VectorMind files or identifiers.
